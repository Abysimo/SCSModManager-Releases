# Screenshots

## Provenance and quality

These six English/Dark screenshots show the actual **1.0.0-rc.2-local.24**
application before the stable release. They use installed mods, profiles and game
data. Identifying details are covered by visible opaque redactions; the controls,
results and status messages are not reconstructed. A still image does not prove
that a displayed operation was subsequently applied or worked in-game.

The images are window captures, approximately 1632–1638 pixels
wide and 1240–1246 high. Some long names/paths and lower content are clipped by the
application viewport; no text has been reconstructed. The owner applied opaque black
redactions to identifying fields. PNG text metadata is removed; encoded image data,
colour information, dimensions, DPI and the supplied redacted pixels are preserved.
